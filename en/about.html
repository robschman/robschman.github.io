About us

THE STORY BEHIND THE APP

Who's behind this?

Beauty Routine App is an independent indie project from Austria. No corporation, no investor pressure, no data-harvesting business model — just an honest, free app.

My name is Robert Thalhammer. I'm building the Beauty Routine App as a small-business owner ("Kleinunternehmer") in Austria. The real inspiration for this app came from my daughter Zoe. She was the one who kept searching for routine apps — apps where she could actually create, customise and adjust things to her own needs. And of course: free. What she found on the market was either packed with ads, hidden behind a subscription, or so clunky that she'd give up after two days.

Zoe is a teenager and is just getting into the phase where you start paying attention to yourself — to health, to beauty, to your own well-being. What was important to us as a family: that she finds a healthy, balanced path with it. So not too much make-up, but rather taking good care of her skin from the start. Gentle cleansing, moisturising, sun protection. Looking after the hair regularly instead of over-styling. A few simple, healthy habits you pick up early and keep for life.

That's exactly what the app was supposed to be: a reliable little companion that reminds you of the good stuff — not one that keeps pushing the next expensive product on you.

So I started building one — initially just for Zoe. She helped from day one: what's missing, what's annoying, what she would actually use. From the many conversations with her, step by step, what you see today emerged. Today the app is for everyone who's looking for the same thing she was back then.

Why this app?

Beauty isn't the point. Consistency is the point. A 5-step skincare routine doesn't do much if you do it twice a month. A 2-step routine that you actually pull off every night changes things.

That's what the Beauty Routine App is for: it's your little reminder companion. Morning check: "Did I do cleansing, serum, and sunscreen?" Evening the same, plus a mask once a week, plus a hair appointment in 6 weeks. All at a glance. All checkable. All yours.

The app doesn't replace a dermatologist or a beauty advisor. It's also not an AI telling you what to buy. It's a tool that helps you stick with it — no matter what products you use, no matter what skin type you have.

What matters to us

  • Free stays free. The app is funded by discreet, GDPR-compliant ads (Google AdSense) — never by hidden purchases or premium walls in front of the core features.
  • GDPR-compliant and transparent. We only set cookies after your explicit consent via the cookie banner. We don't sell data. Period.
  • Stored locally. Your routine, your appointments, your checklists live in your browser's Local Storage — not on our servers. We don't see any of it.
  • No account, no email required. You can use the app without us ever knowing who you are.
  • Made in Austria. Hosted in Europe, built with European privacy in mind.
  • Indie and honest. If something doesn't work, write me directly — I read every email myself.

How is this funded?

Three simple answers:

1. Ads via Google AdSense — small ads, clearly labelled, only after your cookie consent. That's the main source of revenue.

2. Affiliate links to skincare products and beauty brands — if you buy through a link, I earn a small commission. You pay not a cent more. Affiliate links in the footer are always marked with an asterisk (*).

3. My own money. The app has been running for months, long before it ever made any revenue. That's normal for an indie project — and a risk I gladly carry because I believe in the idea.

Who is the app for?

Anyone who wants to take their skincare a bit more seriously but doesn't have time for complicated apps:

• Beginners who are building a proper skincare routine for the first time.
• Routine hoppers who try a new system every three weeks — and need someone to remind them of the basics.
• Skincare enthusiasts who additionally want to keep track of hair, beauty and waxing appointments.
• Men and women alike. The app has a "Boy Mode" with blue-purple colours instead of pink — because skincare has no gender.

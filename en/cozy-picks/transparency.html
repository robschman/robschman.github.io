Transparency at CozyVibe

HOW OUR RECOMMENDATIONS WORK

Last updated: 3 September 2026 · Version 1.1

Every product is a recommendation with an affiliate link

All products on cozy-picks and in our pins carry affiliate links to Amazon: if you buy through such a link, we receive a small commission – the price does not change for you. That is why these posts are labelled as "advertising".

What we do not do

No paid placements of individual brands, no sponsored posts and no free test products in exchange for a review. We choose the products according to our own assessment; the commission is the same for every product and does not influence the selection.

Images and purchase

Product photos are in part created with AI and are labelled accordingly. A purchase is always concluded with Amazon, never with us – Amazon's terms apply to price, delivery, withdrawal and warranty.

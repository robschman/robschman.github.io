PRIVACY POLICY PURSUANT TO ART. 13 GDPR

The Beauty Routine App stores the following data exclusively locally on your device (browser localStorage):

  • The name you enter
  • Your settings (gender, hair color, language)
  • Your completed routine steps
  • The appointments you enter
  • Your cookie consent (bra_cookie_consent)
✅ This app data does NOT leave your device. We have no access to it.

✅ The transfer to Google in the USA is based on an EU adequacy decision, so there is no special risk for you.

The right to data portability applies only to data that you have provided to us on a server. A separate export by us is technically not possible and also not necessary.

For data held at Google (Analytics, only after consent), you can request an export via Google Takeout.

Delete local data: App data (name, settings) directly in the app settings or via browser settings, then website data, then clear.
